SYPHU-China / Research page identity / 9 October 2026

Scope
Software, Model, the Wet Lab pages and ordinary article pages share
ResearchPageShell: one header family, typography, reading width, chapter rhythm,
desktop contents and a keyboard-accessible mobile contents panel.
The existing Home and Team scene layouts remain separate formats.

Art direction
The starting point is the team's existing crowned mint-green EcN character in
assets/story/artist/ecn-original.png. A consistent dark-teal outline, mint body,
cream paper and pale yellow accents connects three chapter scenes:
software.png - reporter analysis at a laptop;
model.png - a symbolic model-building scene;
wet-lab.png - goggles, a culture dish and a vial.
These are decorative chapter illustrations, not measured results or diagrams
from a scientific simulation. Page titles remain live, selectable HTML text.
The broad italic display title, restrained serif chapter headings and readable
sans-serif body belong to one shared CSS implementation.

Final prompt set
Shared prompt:
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original SYPHU-China iGEM website
section header illustration, genuine transparent cutout. Reference image is
the team's EXISTING mascot, not an edit target. Preserve its identity:
elongated rounded light mint-green bacterium, thick dark teal outline, three
pale yellow spots, simple navy smiling closed eyes, small yellow crown. Keep
the distinctive handmade flat 2D ink-and-gouache / printed paper aesthetic.
Color palette sage, soft mint, deep forest-teal, warm cream and small pale yellow
accents. Create a carefully composed, expressive single illustration rather
than generic stock art. No backdrop, no frame, no text or numbers, no letters,
no gradient glow, no realistic lab photograph, no 3D rendering, no watermarks.
All objects contained with generous transparent padding. Intended at 350px tall
beside an oversized page title.

software.png subject:
The crowned microbe is a small friendly analyst leaning over a cream and sage
open laptop. The laptop screen contains only a simple dark green descending
curve and three round observations (no axes numbers). Beside it rests a little
notebook with one curved line, not text. Three-quarter side view, one coherent
compact scene, recognizable mascot. Do not add hands resembling a human person;
use two tiny mitten-shaped simple appendages.

model.png subject:
The crowned microbe stands as a curious model builder beside a simple
sculptural curve made from one flexible deep teal arc with three pale yellow
nodes on a low cream plinth; it lightly holds a small triangular mint ruler.
One coherent compact scene, mascot recognizable. No pseudo-scientific equations,
no text; this is symbolic chapter art, not a scientific data plot.

wet-lab.png subject:
The crowned microbe wears tiny clear protective goggles and looks down at a
transparent shallow petri dish on a low cream workbench. The dish contains three
small green colony-like dots. A single small capped mint vial stands at the side.
One coherent compact laboratory vignette. This is a friendly symbolic
illustration, not experimental evidence. No human characters, no lettering.
